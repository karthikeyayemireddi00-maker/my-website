Kedarnath Temple

Kedarnath is one of the twelve sacred Jyotirlinga shrines dedicated to Lord Shiva. Tucked safely away in the striking Garhwal region of Uttarakhand at an altitude of 3,583 meters, this ancient marvel stands resilient amidst snow-blanketed peaks, remaining an eternal beacon for pilgrims chasing profound spiritual awakening.

Shaivism • Jyotirlinga

Badrinath Temple

Badrinath counts among India's elite Char Dham pilgrimage pillars, standing tall in honor of Lord Vishnu. Resting serenely within Uttarakhand's Chamoli district at an elevation of 3,133 meters, its bright colors clash beautifully against the raw Himalayan backdrop, whispering timeless tales of devotion.

Vaishnavism • Char Dham

Somnath Temple

Somnath marks the historic first of the twelve sacred Jyotirlinga monuments dedicated to Lord Shiva. Perched gracefully on the edge of the Arabian Sea in Gujarat, this crown jewel of architecture stands as a legendary emblem of rebirth, resilience, and unyielding spiritual grandeur across centuries.

Shaivism • Jyotirlinga

Kashi Vishwanath Temple

Kashi Vishwanath stands as the pulsating spiritual heart of Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh. Dedicated to Lord Shiva, this supreme Jyotirlinga shrine lines the banks of the sacred Ganges. It serves as an intricate architectural wonder and a terminal point for seekers looking to cross the threshold of cosmic liberation.

Shaivism • Jyotirlinga

Tirupati Temple

Tirumala Venkateswara Temple is a magnificent hill shrine dedicated to Lord Venkateswara, an incarnation of Lord Vishnu. Set atop the scenic Saptagiri hills of Andhra Pradesh, it ranks as one of the most visited and culturally rich religious epicenters across the globe.

Vaishnavism • Divya Desam

Vaishno Devi Temple

Vaishno Devi is a famous mountain sanctuary honoring the divine goddess Vaishno Devi, a powerful manifestation of the Supreme Mother. Resting safely within the Trikuta Mountains of Jammu and Kashmir, it calls out to millions who complete the arduous hillside trek to receive her protective gaze.

Shaktism • Holy Cave

Puri Jagannath Temple

Puri Jagannath is an ancestral fortress of faith dedicated to Lord Jagannath, a loving form of Lord Vishnu. Located along the coastal winds of Odisha, this Char Dham landmark is universally celebrated for its colossal architecture and the legendary, vibrant colors of the annual Rath Yatra festival.

Vaishnavism • Char Dham

Mahakaleshwar Temple

Mahakaleshwar is a unique, powerhouse Jyotirlinga situated on the banks of the Kshipra river in Ujjain, Madhya Pradesh. Famed for its rare south-facing idol and the mystical, daily Bhasma Aarti ritual, this sanctuary honors Shiva as the ultimate ruler of time and dissolution.

Shaivism • Jyotirlinga

KanakaDurga Temple

KanakaDurga Temple commands a majestic presence atop the Indrakeeladri hill in Vijayawada, Andhra Pradesh. Overlooking the Krishna River, this ancient fort of Shaktism celebrates the golden-hued goddess, drawing vast crowds who climb to witness her radiant grace, especially during Navratri.

Shaktism • Hill Shrine

Omkareshwar Temple

Omkareshwar is a highly revered Jyotirlinga sanctuary located on Mandhata island within the Narmada River in Madhya Pradesh. Distinctively structured, the very geometry of the island naturally forms the shape of the sacred Hindu OM symbol, rendering it a geographical marvel of cosmic design.

Shaivism • Jyotirlinga

Meenakshi Temple

The historical Meenakshi Sundareswarar Temple thrives in the heart of Madurai, Tamil Nadu. Dedicated symmetrically to Goddess Meenakshi and Lord Shiva, this vast temple labyrinth stands as a legendary pinnacle of Dravidian design, characterized by its towering, heavily sculpted Gopurams.

Shaktism / Shaivism

Kaamakhya Temple

Kamakhya Temple is a deeply esoteric Shakti Peetha resting on the Nilachal hills of Guwahati, Assam. Standing as the core center of complex Tantric traditions, this sanctuary honors the creative cycle of the cosmos, world-renowned for its powerful atmosphere and unique seasonal festivals.

Shaktism • Shakti Peetha
